<objective>
Make usage settlement an explicit terminal side effect: completed requests settle once; unavailable final Usage is recorded as `missing_usage`; all non-completed outcomes do not debit; and persistence failures become observable without changing the client terminal result.

Deliverable: a focused settlement module below the file-size limit, sanitized `slog` warning handling for every previously ignored persistence error, and deterministic tests proving debit/no-debit/observability behavior.
</objective>

**Preconditions:** `27-01` supplies immutable outcome/finalizer; `27-02` has split stream services and installed lifecycle callbacks. The current `settle` code's ignored repository and aggregator errors are the regression target.

**Dependencies:** Requires `27-01` and `27-02`. May run in parallel with `27-03`. `27-05` validates combined behavior.

**Duration:** 1 engineer-day. No reconciliation job, retry queue, billing schema migration, or pricing change is in scope.

**Test environment:** Local Go unit tests with fake Usage repository, fake settlement repository, fake cost aggregator, synthetic final Usage/no Usage, and a captured `slog` handler. No database/server required.
</execution_context>

<acceptance_criteria>
1. `Settle` is called exactly once only for accepted `completed` outcomes with valid final Usage.
2. Completed outcomes without final Usage write one `missing_usage` record and do not debit.
3. Provider error, cancellation, policy rejection, and abnormal outcomes never call settlement or usage-log persistence, even when Usage exists.
4. Failures from settlement, missing-Usage logging, and cost aggregation produce exactly one sanitized warning/metric and are never silently ignored.
5. Persistence failure does not change terminal response, block admission release, or cause retry/double debit.
</acceptance_criteria>
